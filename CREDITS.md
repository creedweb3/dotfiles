## Built on

- [Hyprland](https://hyprland.org) - the compositor
- [Quickshell](https://quickshell.org) - bar, panels, lock screen and settings
- [wallust](https://codeberg.org/explosion-mental/wallust) - colours from the wallpaper
- [JetBrains Mono](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/) (Nerd Font build) - the font
- [Papirus](https://github.com/PapirusDevelopmentTeam/papirus-icon-theme) and
  [adw-gtk3](https://github.com/lassekongo83/adw-gtk3) - icons and GTK theme
